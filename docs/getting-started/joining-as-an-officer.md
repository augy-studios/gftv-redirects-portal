# Joining as an Officer

To create short links on gftv.asia, you need an account with **Editor** access. This page explains how to register and what to expect during the approval process.

## Before you register

gftv.asia uses a **pre-approval system** to maintain the integrity of the platform. In most cases, an Admin will add your email address to the pre-approved list before you register. This ensures your account is activated with the correct role the moment you sign up.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you're a new GFTV officer, ask your team lead or an Admin to pre-approve your email before you attempt to register.
{% endhint %}

If your email has not been pre-approved, your account will be created with **Pending** status and will require manual approval by an Admin before you can do anything.

## Registering an account

1. Go to [gftv.asia](https://gftv.asia) and click **Register** (or navigate to the sign-up page from the menu).
2. Enter your desired **username** (3–30 characters; letters, numbers, and underscores only).
3. Enter your **email address**.
4. Choose a strong **password**.
5. Click **Register**.

### Username rules

- Must be 3–30 characters long.
- May contain letters (`a–z`, `A–Z`), numbers (`0–9`), and underscores (`_`).
- Must be unique — no two accounts can share a username.

### Password requirements

gftv.asia enforces strong passwords using an entropy-based strength check rather than simple length or complexity rules. A password must meet a minimum entropy score to be accepted. Use a passphrase or a password manager to generate a strong password.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Write down your password or save it in a password manager. Admins can reset your password if you lose it, but they will not be able to tell you what it was.
{% endhint %}

## Account statuses

After registering, your account will be in one of the following states:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active (Editor)** | Your email was pre-approved as Editor. You can create and manage links immediately. |
| **Active (Viewer)** | Your email was pre-approved as Viewer. You can browse the directory but not create links. |
| **Pending** | Your email was not pre-approved. An Admin must manually approve your account before you can log in. |

## What happens after approval?

Once an Admin approves your account and grants you the Editor role, you can:

- Log in at [gftv.asia](https://gftv.asia).
- Access your **Dashboard** to create and manage short links.
- Appear as a verified officer in the public [Directory](../directory/browsing-the-directory.md).

## Changing your role

Role changes (e.g. being upgraded from Viewer to Editor) are made by Admins. If you believe your role should be different, contact an Admin directly.
