# What is gftv.asia?

**gftv.asia** is the official short link platform for GFTV (Global Furry Television). It lets verified GFTV officers create short, trustworthy links under the `gftv.asia` domain that community members can click on with confidence.

## The trust model

Generic link shorteners (like bit.ly or tinyurl.com) let anyone create short links — including bad actors. This makes it difficult for community members to know whether a shortened link is genuine or a phishing attempt.

gftv.asia solves this by **restricting link creation to approved GFTV officers only**. Every link at `gftv.asia/...` was created by a real, vetted person on the GFTV team. Community members who see a `gftv.asia` link shared in an official GFTV channel can trust it came from a legitimate source.

{% hint style="success" %}
Think of `gftv.asia` links the same way you'd think of links on an official `.gov` domain — the domain itself is the trust signal.
{% endhint %}

## How short links work

When someone visits a `gftv.asia` short link (for example, `gftv.asia/discord`), they are immediately redirected to the destination URL set by the link's owner.

If a link has been deactivated by its owner, visitors are redirected to the GFTV blog instead of reaching the original destination.

## Roles on the platform

There are three types of accounts on gftv.asia:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Browse the public link directory and view profiles. No link creation. |
| **Editor** | Everything a Viewer can do, plus create, edit, and manage short links. |
| **Admin** | Everything an Editor can do, plus manage users, approve registrations, and administer any link on the platform. |

New accounts start as **Pending** until an Admin approves them. Admins can also pre-approve email addresses before a person even registers.

## Who is gftv.asia for?

gftv.asia is for **GFTV officers** — staff, volunteers, and other personnel operating under the GFTV banner. It is not a general-purpose link shortener for the public.

Community members who are not officers can still visit the public [Directory](../directory/browsing-the-directory.md) to browse all published links.

## Open source

The gftv.asia portal is open source. You can view the code, report issues, and contribute at the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/augy-studios/gftv-redirects-portal).
