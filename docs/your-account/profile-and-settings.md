# Profile & Settings

Your profile is publicly visible to anyone who visits gftv.asia. It shows your role, the links you've created, and any social links you've added. This page explains how to update your profile and manage your account settings.

## Accessing your settings

1. Log in to [gftv.asia](https://gftv.asia).
2. Click on your avatar or username in the navigation bar.
3. Select **Settings** (or **Profile**) from the dropdown.

## Display name

Your **display name** is the name shown on your public profile and on link cards in the Directory. It can be different from your username.

To update it:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Edit the **Display Name** field.
3. Click **Save**.

## Avatar

Your profile picture is displayed on your public profile and alongside your links in the Directory.

To upload or change your avatar:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Click your current avatar (or the upload area).
3. Select an image file from your device.
4. Click **Save**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Uploaded images are automatically converted to **WebP** format and compressed for faster loading. Very large images will be resized automatically.
{% endhint %}

Supported input formats include JPEG, PNG, and WebP. The recommended upload size is at least 200×200 pixels.

## Social links

You can add up to several **social links** to your public profile — for example, links to your Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or other platforms relevant to your GFTV work.

Each social link has two parts:
- **Label** — a short display name for the link (e.g. `Twitter`, `Portfolio`).
- **URL** — the full link (e.g. `https://twitter.com/yourhandle`).

To add or edit social links:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Social Links** section.
3. Click **Add Link**, then fill in the label and URL.
4. Click **Save**.

To remove a social link, click the **×** or **Remove** button next to it.

## Changing your password

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Password** section.
3. Enter your **current password**.
4. Enter and confirm your **new password**.
5. Click **Update Password**.

The password strength indicator will show you how strong your new password is as you type. Your new password must meet the platform's minimum entropy requirement before it can be saved.

{% hint style="warning" %}
After changing your password, your current session remains active but other active sessions will be invalidated. If you want to end all sessions, use **Log out from all devices**.
{% endhint %}

## Username and email

Your **username** and **email address** cannot be changed by yourself. Contact an Admin if you need either of these updated.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my profile visible without logging in?**
Yes. Your public profile (display name, avatar, role badge, social links, and statistics) is visible to anyone who visits your profile URL.

**Can I hide my links from my public profile?**
No. All active links created by your account are associated with your profile and visible in the Directory.
