# Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your gftv.asia account at any time. This action is irreversible.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Account deletion is **permanent**. Your account, all your links, and associated data will be removed and cannot be recovered. Before deleting, consider transferring ownership of any important links to a colleague.
{% endhint %}

## Before you delete

Take a moment to consider the following:

- **Your links will be deleted.** All short links you own will be removed. Visitors who follow those links will no longer be redirected anywhere. If these links are published in community materials, they will become dead.
- **Transfer important links first.** If any of your links are still in active use, [transfer their ownership](../managing-links/editing-a-link.md) to another Editor before deleting your account.
- **Your profile will disappear.** Your public profile, avatar, and social links will no longer be accessible.

## How to delete your account

1. Log in to [gftv.asia](https://gftv.asia).
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section at the bottom of the page.
4. Click **Delete Account**.
5. Enter your **current password** to confirm.
6. Click **Confirm Delete**.

Your account and all associated data are removed immediately.

## What is deleted

| Data | Deleted? |
|---|---|
| Account (username, email, display name) | Yes |
| Password and session tokens | Yes |
| Avatar | Yes |
| Social links | Yes |
| Short links you own | Yes |
| Ownership requests you submitted | Yes |
| 2FA settings and backup codes | Yes |
| Trusted device records | Yes |

## What is not deleted

View count data and anonymised visit records for your links may be retained in aggregated platform statistics even after your account is removed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can an Admin restore my account after deletion?**
No. Deletion is permanent. There is no recovery option.

**What if I just want to take a break from the platform?**
You do not need to delete your account. You can simply log out and stop using it. Your links will remain active and owned by you. Alternatively, you can [deactivate your links](../managing-links/link-status.md) without deleting your account.

**Can an Admin delete my account on my behalf?**
Yes. Admins can delete any user account from the admin panel. The same permanent data removal applies.
